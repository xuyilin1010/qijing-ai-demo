## panorama.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one original ultra-wide panoramic environmental art asset for an unbranded travel exploration interactive long-scroll web experience. Output a wide landscape image, ideally 3840 x 1280, aspect ratio 3:1. This is an immersive cinematic photorealistic landscape plate, not a mockup: NO text, typography, logo, borders, panels, UI, people or products. An uninterrupted believable dreamlike wilderness transitions smoothly from left to right: at far left a calm deep cobalt ocean at blue hour and a distant low horizon with a thin warm dawn glow; then along the center-left natural ochre sand dunes with fine rippled sand, dramatic graceful curving ridges; center-right massive warm amber stratified sandstone canyon with a fine winding blue river in its depths; far right distant mountains become dark silhouettes under an expansive navy night sky with delicate stars. Maintain one continuous landform and sky, horizon roughly 40% from top, detailed foreground in bottom half and broad dark blue negative sky in top half for later editable headings. Rich navy and ultramarine blues, restrained warm gold grazing light on rock and sand, amber dust, natural photographic texture, atmospheric haze and depth, understated sophisticated editorial landscape, no glowing fantasy portals, no neon trails, no city. Extremely refined photographic detail in geology and sand with soft low light. The transitions must be continuous rather than separate rectangular scenes. The panorama will move behind minimal white Chinese text added separately in code.
